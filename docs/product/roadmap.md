# Public Product Roadmap

> Planning information only. Dates, scope, sequencing, and delivery are subject to change.

## Phase 0 — Product and architecture definition

- Define modular domain boundaries
- Establish security, data-minimization, and audit principles
- Produce a chain-selection scorecard
- Document mock-versus-live boundaries

## Phase 1 — Synthetic lifecycle MVP

- Account and organization scaffolding
- Mock NovaID verification state
- Synthetic asset registration
- NovaRegistry record lifecycle
- Mock NovaDeed artifact lifecycle
- Operator dashboard with lifecycle history
- Append-only audit-event and transactional-outbox foundations

## Phase 2 — Controlled integration readiness

- Managed authentication and tenant controls
- Persisted mock identity workflows
- Policy decision records
- Test environments and operational observability
- Independent security review planning

## Phase 3 — Regulated integration assessment

- Jurisdictional legal and regulatory analysis
- KYC/KYB, legal-record, payment, and custody-provider evaluation
- Data residency, retention, privacy, and incident-response design
- Chain and oracle integration prototypes only after approval

## Phase 4 — Production eligibility gates

No production deployment, token issuance, stablecoin activity, real payment processing, or mainnet operation should occur until legal, compliance, security, operational, governance, and reserve requirements are independently approved.
