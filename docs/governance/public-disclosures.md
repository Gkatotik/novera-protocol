# Public Disclosures and Governance Position

## Current status

Novera Protocol is an early-stage product and architecture initiative. Statements in this repository describe intended directions and do not guarantee delivery, availability, regulatory status, market acceptance, or commercial outcomes.

## NOVA

- The contemplated fixed maximum supply is **1,000,000,000 NOVA tokens**.
- No NOVA token has been created, issued, offered, sold, distributed, or listed.
- No statement here is an offer, sale, solicitation, or recommendation involving NOVA or any other financial instrument.
- Any future issuance would require explicit governance, legal, regulatory, technical, security, and operational approval.

## NovaStable

- NovaStable is an intended **1:1 CAD-pegged** concept.
- No NovaStable asset has been created, issued, reserved, marketed, or deployed.
- Any future stable-value product would require legal classification, reserve design, independent assurance, custody/banking arrangements, compliance controls, redemption design, and risk governance before consideration.

## Governance principles

Future governance design remains open. The public posture is to use staged approvals, documented decision records, least-privilege access, independent review where appropriate, and clear separation between product demonstrations and regulated activity.

## No professional advice

Nothing in this repository is legal, financial, investment, tax, accounting, or regulatory advice. Prospective investors, counterparties, and users should obtain independent professional advice.
