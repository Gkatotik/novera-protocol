# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to maintain a professional, respectful, and constructive public discussion space for Novera Protocol.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate in good faith, focus on evidence and ideas, respect privacy and confidentiality, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Do not publish confidential information, credentials, personal data, deceptive investment claims, unlawful content, or material that creates avoidable security, legal, or regulatory risk.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when conduct conflicts with this policy. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s contact channel.
